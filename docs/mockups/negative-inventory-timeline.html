Negative inventory — what shipping before you receive does to cost

Sometimes the warehouse is ahead of the paperwork: goods arrive and leave before anyone types the receipt, so a shipment is entered while the system still reads 0 on hand. ERPNext will allow that once allow_negative_stock is on — the question this page is trying to make visual is what it costs you to allow it, and when that cost appears.

Short version: the shipment still books a cost, but at a guessed rate — and whether that guess ever gets corrected depends on one thing only: the posting date you give the receipt. Date it when the goods arrived and ERPNext restates the shipment itself. Date it when you typed it and the error is quietly moved into a different month instead. Sections B and C are the same documents with that one field changed.

Discovery notes: OI-178 (this page) and OI-177 (the cost-layer panel that would show it). ✅ Every number in sections A–D was read back off this sandbox on 2026-09-26, not reasoned about — an earlier draft of this page got section B wrong in exactly the way the experiment disproved.

C · Out of order, receipt backdated to when the goods really arrived this one self-corrects

🔴 The repost is asynchronous. The Repost Item Valuation lands Queued and a background worker runs it. For a moment after submitting the receipt, the shipment still reads 60.00 — so a costing panel that reads straight after a save can show a figure that is about to change. It has to say so rather than present it as settled.

E · Reviewing it periodically what vanilla already gives you
There is no “negative inventory review” report in ERPNext. There are four existing reports that between them cover the job, and the gap is that none of them is framed as here is what is negative and here is how to clear it.
ReportWhat it answersGap for a monthly review
Stock Balance Balance qty and value per item/warehouse — negatives show as negative rows No “negatives only” filter; you sort and eyeball
Stock Ledger The movement history behind one item, with running balance Per item. Fine once you know which item
FIFO Queue vs Qty After Transaction Where the layer queue disagrees with the recorded balance Diagnostic, not a to-do list; names rows, not causes
Stock Ledger Invariant Check Whether the ledger's own arithmetic still holds The strongest audit of the four, and the least readable

A monthly review is really three questions, and the reports answer them in this order: what is negative right now (Stock Balance) → which shipment went out before its receipt (Stock Ledger on that item) → backdate the receipt to the date the goods arrived, which per section C makes ERPNext restate the cost itself. That third step is the clearing action, and it is the part no report tells you to take.

F · What the shell would say, and when proposal
Consistent with invariant 7 and with how void-and-amend already handles blockers: name the consequence, never enforce it. The warehouse is right and the paperwork is late — refusing the shipment would be the system contradicting a fact. So nothing here blocks; it labels.
  • At the moment of shipping “This ships 2 you have not received yet. Its cost is a placeholder of 60.00 a unit, taken from the item record because there is no receipt to read.” On the Delivery Note, before submit. Not a block — a label.
  • When the receipt is finally entered — the one that actually matters “2 of this went out on Aug 1 before anything was received. Date this receipt Jul 22, when the goods arrived, and that shipment's cost is corrected automatically. Date it today and 20.00 of cost stays in the wrong month.” This is the only prompt on the page that changes the books for the better, because backdating is the whole fix and nothing currently suggests it.
  • On the cost-layer panel (OI-177), while the balance is still negative A −2 row shown as owed-to-the-ledger rather than as stock, its rate marked guess so nobody totals it as cost, and “a repost may still change this” whenever an unfinished Repost Item Valuation exists for the item. The panel reads stock_queue; a negative bin is not a layer and must not be drawn as one.
  • If no rate can be found at all The fallback walk ends at zero and the shipment books no cost whatsoever, quietly. A brand-new SKU shipped before its first receipt has no valuation rate, no standard rate and no buying price — that case deserves a louder warning than the others.

Where the behaviour comes from (ERPNext v16.32.1, read from source, not guessed): the fallback walk is get_fallback_rate in stock_ledger.py — last valuation rate, then Item.valuation_rate, then standard_rate, then a buying Item Price. The negative layer is valuation.py’s remove_stock (“stock finished, qty still remains to be withdrawn — negative stock, keep in as a negative bin”). The setting is Stock Settings.allow_negative_stock, with a per-item override reached through is_negative_stock_allowed(item_code=…), so this can be opened for the few SKUs that need it instead of site-wide. ERPNext warns about the restatement itself and links its own stock adjustment COGS with negative stock page.

How far back does a repost go, and is there a cutoff? Out of the box, none — a backdated entry reposts every later movement for that item and warehouse however far back it reaches, and a purchase dated after the sale raises no repost at all, however soon it follows. Three settings exist to impose a limit, all unset on this instance: Stock Settings.stock_frozen_upto (a hard date), stock_frozen_upto_days (a rolling window) — both bypassable only by stock_auth_role — and role_allowed_to_create_edit_back_dated_transactions, which restricts who may post earlier than an item's last movement at all.

Which rate fills the negative? The rate of the last layer it consumed, or the item master fallback when there was no layer at all — never a future receipt's rate. remove_stock appends [-qty, outgoing_rate or fifo_bin[RATE]]. So a later cheaper purchase does not retroactively set the guess; only a repost changes it, and only a backdated entry raises one.

What is invented here: the 60.00 stale master rate, the document numbers, and every word in section F. The mechanism and all the arithmetic are real — the rates are chosen so the 20.00 is easy to follow. Section E's report list is complete for erpnext/stock/report at this tag.
