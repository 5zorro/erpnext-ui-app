Negative inventory — what shipping before you receive does to cost

Sometimes the warehouse is ahead of the paperwork: goods arrive and leave before anyone types the receipt, so a shipment is entered while the system still reads 0 on hand. ERPNext will allow that once allow_negative_stock is on — the question this page is trying to make visual is what it costs you to allow it, and when that cost appears.

Short version: the shipment still books a cost, but at a guessed rate, and when the receipt finally lands ERPNext reaches back and rewrites it. A document's booked cost changes after submit, without anybody touching the document. Discovery notes: OI-178 (this page) and OI-177 (the cost-layer panel that would show it).

D · What the shell would say, and when proposal
Consistent with invariant 7 and with how void-and-amend already handles blockers: name the consequence, never enforce it. The warehouse is right and the paperwork is late — refusing the shipment would be the system contradicting a fact. So nothing here blocks; it labels.
  • At the moment of shipping “This ships 2 you have not received yet. Its cost is a placeholder of 60.00 a unit and will be restated when the receipt is entered.” On the Delivery Note, before submit. Not a block — a label.
  • On the cost-layer panel (OI-177), while it is still negative A −2 row shown as owed-to-the-ledger rather than as stock, with its rate marked guess so nobody totals it as if it were cost. The panel reads stock_queue; it must not present a negative bin as a layer.
  • After the repost “Cost restated 120.00 → 100.00 when Item Receipt PR-00042 was entered on day 5.” The one line an auditor actually needs, and the one ERPNext does not currently surface anywhere near the document.
  • If no rate can be found at all The fallback walk ends at zero, and the shipment books no cost whatsoever — quietly. That is the case worth a louder warning than the others. A brand-new SKU shipped before its first receipt has no valuation rate, no standard rate and no buying price.

Where the behaviour comes from (ERPNext v16.32.1, read from source, not guessed): the fallback walk is get_fallback_rate in stock_ledger.py — last valuation rate, then Item.valuation_rate, then standard_rate, then a buying Item Price. The negative layer is valuation.py’s remove_stock (“stock finished, qty still remains to be withdrawn — negative stock, keep in as a negative bin”). The setting is Stock Settings.allow_negative_stock, with a per-item override reached through is_negative_stock_allowed(item_code=…), so this can be opened for the few SKUs that need it instead of site-wide. ERPNext warns about the restatement itself and links its own stock adjustment COGS with negative stock page.

What is invented here: the 60.00 stale master rate, the document numbers, the day spacing, and every word in section D. The mechanism is real; the numbers are chosen to make the 20.00 restatement easy to see. The exact bin arithmetic at the instant the balance crosses zero has not been verified against this instance — section B steps from −2 to +1 to stay clear of it.
