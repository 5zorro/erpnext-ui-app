Bowtie trace

One set of links between documents, three ways to look at it. Statement is a Find page in vendor-statement mode. Bowtie puts one or more documents in focus and flows everything they came from to the left and everything they led to on the right. Gantt is the same documents on a calendar, shading the gap between the date on the paper and the day it was posted.

What this mockup assumes (5zorro's answers, 2026-09-28)
  • Landed cost. Every bill has at least one item line, and the bill's charges (freight etc.) are spread over its lines by each line's share of the item total. Shown under a bill in focus. This is not ERPNext's Landed Cost Voucher, which has not been used or dogfooded here.
  • Who submitted. Shown as the document's creator (right about 9 times in 10). To come back to: read the actual submitter from the change history and show both when they differ.
  • Ageing is by the vendor's invoice date: the vendor's invoice is the legal document.
  • Ribbon width is amount.
  • Payments covering several bills: the part that isn't this bill's flows in from one blank Other payables box, and the payment walks to those bills only when it's in focus itself.
  • Item mismatch (added while building): a line that names the document it came from, when that document has no such item, gets a ⚠ on its ribbon and on the card. Clicking it opens the detail. Clean rows show nothing. The same check would catch a line asking for more than its source had.
Two dates on every card: which ones
  • Doc = the date on the paper. Bill: the vendor's invoice date. Payment: the check date. Receipt: the day the goods arrived. PO, Sales Order, Estimate, Invoice: the document's date.
  • Posted = the date it reached the books. Bill and payment: ERPNext's posting date. Receipt: the day it was keyed in. Order-type documents have no posting date, so the day they were submitted.
  • 🔴 Open question: is that what "date posted / date of transaction" meant? For a receipt, ERPNext's posting date is the arrival date, so its "posted" would have to come from the created stamp.
Other open questions
  • Two in focus, but no path between them (a bill and the customer's payment, say): shown as the two lineages side by side, with the Sales Order where they meet. Is that right, or should the bowtie say "these two are not connected"?
  • Hidden branches. A Sales Order in the bowtie of a bill also led to a customer invoice. That branch is left out and counted in a small "+1 not in focus" note on the card. Or should it show as a stub?
  • Credits and returns (the vendor credit on the statement) aren't linked into the bowtie yet. A return points at the bill it reverses, so it would flow out to the right of that bill.
  • Drag to add focus is drag-onto-the-bar here. Box-select across cards could come later.
What the sample data needs before this can be built for real
  • Bills that draw from two or more POs/receipts: the sandbox had none, so nothing fanned in.
  • Bills with NIC lines and charges mixed with sourced lines, so landed cost has something to spread.
  • Lag between the paper date and the posting date (the seed posts most on the same day, so the Gantt would draw dots and no bars).
  • A PO line linked to a Sales Order that doesn't carry that item, and one asking for more than the order had, so the mismatch check has a positive case.
  • A few documents created by one user and submitted by another, for the submitter follow-up.
