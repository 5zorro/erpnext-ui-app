Bowtie trace

One set of links between documents, three ways to look at it. Statement is a Find page in vendor-statement mode. Bowtie puts one or more documents in focus and flows everything they came from to the left and everything they led to on the right. Gantt is the same documents on a calendar, shading the gap between the date on the paper and the day it was posted.

One row per item, one column per stage — the same links as the bowtie, read as a table, for every Sales Order in focus. Each cell is how many (or, for payments, how much of the bill) reached that stage and what it cost; click it for the documents behind it. A gap is marked on the cell where it shows up. Margin is what the customer was invoiced minus the landed cost of that many, net of anything credited back. In the build each column is one entry in a list of links (which field joins which document), so a new link type — Material Request, drop ship, an advance — fills a column or adds one, and never needs a new drawing rule.
What this mockup assumes (5zorro's answers, 2026-09-28)
  • Landed cost. Every bill has at least one item line, and the bill's charges (freight etc.) are spread over its lines by each line's share of the item total. Shown under a bill in focus. This is not ERPNext's Landed Cost Voucher, which has not been used or dogfooded here.
  • Who submitted. Shown as the document's creator (right about 9 times in 10). To come back to: read the actual submitter from the change history and show both when they differ.
  • Ageing is by the vendor's invoice date: the vendor's invoice is the legal document.
  • Ribbon width is amount.
  • Payments covering several bills: the part that isn't this bill's flows in from one blank Other payables box, and the payment walks to those bills only when it's in focus itself.
  • Item tables on every card, ribbons row to row (5zorro, same day): so one item can be traced through every document. Hover a row to light its path. In the build a row links to its parent row by the ids ERPNext already stores on each line (po_detail, pr_detail, sales_order_item, so_detail); the mockup matches by item code only because its data has no row ids.
  • Quick filters on the Find page (same day): Find Bills opens with Bills on; Vendor credits, Payments, Open POs and Open receipts are one click each. With all of them on the page is the vendor statement, and the Home screen would link straight to that.
  • Gantt colour (same day): every row carries its wash from the start of the calendar to the paper date, then a border on to the posted date, so a same-day document is not a bare dot.
  • Item mismatch (added while building): a line that names the document it came from, when that document has no such item, gets a ⚠ on its ribbon and on the card. Clicking it opens the detail. Clean rows show nothing. The same check would catch a line asking for more than its source had.
Layout rules (5zorro, 2026-09-29)
  • Two lanes: Buy (A/P) on top, Sell (A/R) underneath.
  • Locked on the right, for both lanes: Payment (or a planned one), and the invoice that caused it (Bill / customer Invoice). Every transaction has both.
  • Credits sit in the Payment column — a vendor credit (or a customer credit note) settles a bill the way a payment does. It still links back to the bill it reverses (Return Against), so its ribbon runs from that bill's item row.
  • Everything else is optional and packs right against those two: Receipt, then Order, then Estimate / Material Request. Columns nobody uses disappear.
  • A PO and its Sales Order share the Order column, so the PO's left side meets the Sales Order's left side (PO Item sales_order). That branch is the one ribbon that runs up instead of across.
  • Gross margin fans into the customer invoice's lines the way freight fans into the bill's: price − landed cost of what was bought for that line. Cost bought for the order but never invoiced is called out.
  • An item trace runs to both payments: an item row carries on to its card's Total, and the Sales Order is where the trace turns from the buy side to the sell side.
Other links in the schema (read from this v16 instance)
  • Material Request sits between a Sales Order and a PO: MR Item sales_order / sales_order_item, then PO / Receipt / Bill Item material_request / material_request_item. It belongs in the Estimate · Request column of the Buy lane.
  • Drop ship links the two sides directly, not through the Sales Order row: Sales Order Item purchase_order / purchase_order_item, Receipt Item sales_order, Sales Invoice Item purchase_order. More branches like the SO → PO one.
  • Advances: a Payment Entry Reference can point at a Purchase Order or Sales Order, not only an invoice (and advance_voucher_no records it later being applied). So a payment can be fed straight from an order, skipping the invoice column — "payment always has an invoice" holds only once it is applied.
  • Returns: return_against on Bill, customer Invoice, Receipt and Delivery Note; line-level purchase_invoice_item / sales_invoice_item. A return Receipt would sit with receipts.
  • Amendments: amended_from on every one of them — the void-and-amend chain (…-1). Worth a small "replaces …" marker on the card rather than its own ribbon.
  • Delivery Note (the Sell lane's receipt): against_sales_order / so_detail, and Sales Invoice Item delivery_note / dn_detail. Not used in this shell's flows yet; it would fill the Receipt column in the Sell lane.
  • Also present, probably out of scope: Supplier Quotation → PO, Blanket Order, Pick List, Production Plan, inter-company references, Journal Entries as payments, and Landed Cost Vouchers (which point at receipts).
Two dates on every card: which ones
  • Doc = the date on the paper. Bill: the vendor's invoice date. Payment: the check date. Receipt: the day the goods arrived. PO, Sales Order, Estimate, Invoice: the document's date.
  • Posted = the date it reached the books. Bill and payment: ERPNext's posting date. Receipt: the day it was keyed in. Order-type documents have no posting date, so the day they were submitted.
  • 🔴 Open question: is that what "date posted / date of transaction" meant? For a receipt, ERPNext's posting date is the arrival date, so its "posted" would have to come from the created stamp.
Other open questions
  • Two in focus, but no path between them (a bill and the customer's payment, say): shown as the two lineages side by side, with the Sales Order where they meet. Is that right, or should the bowtie say "these two are not connected"?
  • Hidden branches. A Sales Order in the bowtie of a bill also led to a customer invoice. That branch is left out and counted in a small "+1 not in focus" note on the card. Or should it show as a stub?
  • Credits and returns (the vendor credit on the statement) aren't linked into the bowtie yet. A return points at the bill it reverses, so it would flow out to the right of that bill.
  • Payments on the item trace: a payment draws on a bill's Total row, so tracing an item row stops at the bill. Should the trace carry on to the payment (the item's share of it)?
  • Ribbons that skip columns (Sales Order → customer Invoice) run in a channel under the cards they skip, so they don't look like they come from the PO or receipt in between.
  • Drag to add focus is drag-onto-the-bar here. Box-select across cards could come later.
What the sample data needs before this can be built for real
  • Bills that draw from two or more POs/receipts: the sandbox had none, so nothing fanned in.
  • Bills with NIC lines and charges mixed with sourced lines, so landed cost has something to spread.
  • Lag between the paper date and the posting date (the seed posts most on the same day, so the Gantt would draw dots and no bars).
  • A PO line linked to a Sales Order that doesn't carry that item, and one asking for more than the order had, so the mismatch check has a positive case.
  • A few documents created by one user and submitted by another, for the submitter follow-up.
